# KLEIO Opportunity Visual-Asset Recovery and Application-Enrichment Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-07-29  
**Production project:** KLEIO Supabase (`trekynurdgxgtaaqqtyq`)

## Scope

This audit reviewed the active worldwide opportunity catalog using public and authorised sources only. Official opportunity pages, linked application portals, downloadable guidelines, terms, FAQs, government notices and organiser statements controlled legal, financial, eligibility and submission data. Public social posts, newsletters, forums and community listings were used only for discovery or corroboration.

Private, membership-gated or login-only group content was not accessed or represented as researched.

## Production result

- 105 total opportunity records.
- 93 active open, upcoming or forecasted records.
- 81 records materially audited against current official sources.
- 46 active records now have a specific official or provider visual.
- 47 active records retain a KLEIO fallback after documented research.
- Zero active records remain pending in the image-research queue.
- 24 active records are protected with `needs_review` and excluded from normal artist search.
- 9 records are archived or expired.
- 93 of 93 active records have an application route.
- 81 active records have a normalized required-material list.
- 56 active records have confirmed structured requirement rows.
- 73 active records have confirmed structured eligibility evidence.
- 59 active records have verified financial terms.
- 12 active records have sufficiently verified rights terms.
- 50 active records have a verified public contact email.

## Image-research outcomes

The complete queue contains:

- 42 verified image records.
- 47 unavailable outcomes with documented reasons.
- 3 official candidates awaiting a stable direct asset or further rights resolution.
- 1 remaining pending record belonging to a non-active record; no active opportunity is pending.

All attached visuals have:

- HTTPS image and source URLs.
- Opportunity-specific alt text.
- Attribution.
- A declared origin.
- A non-fabricated rights classification.

`official_publication` means that the organiser publicly published the image. It does not mean KLEIO claims an unrestricted commercial licence.

## Blocking source conflicts

These remain explicitly unresolved:

- Culture Resource: two different birth-date ranges on the official programme page.
- Korean Cultural Centre Australia: contradictory statements about artwork freight coverage.
- OIF: impossible “31 November 2026” implementation end date in the official PDF.
- Arts and Disability Connect: 10 August versus 19 August opening dates across official sources.
- Argentina Cultural Project Consultancies: 30 July versus 31 July registration endpoint.
- ANBA–FNA Composition Prize: original deadline text versus explicit extension.
- UNESCO Jazz Host City: conflicting January and August deadline language on the official page.
- Goethe Africa–Europe Mobility: the PDF names a September cutoff but does not print the exact day shown by the catalog.

KLEIO does not silently normalise material conflicts. Automatic eligibility, readiness or submission stays blocked where the conflict affects the application.

## One-click preparation boundaries

The enriched catalog supports source-backed eligibility comparison, Creative Passport material reuse, opportunity-specific drafting and export-package preparation. KLEIO still does not:

- infer sensitive identity attributes;
- sign declarations;
- accept contracts or rights clauses;
- invent references or eligibility;
- bypass portals, authentication or CAPTCHAs;
- submit without explicit artist approval;
- claim native submission where only an external portal or email route exists.

## Validation

Production validation confirmed:

- No duplicate `(source_id, external_id)` records.
- Cycle-specific NEA indemnity titles are distinct.
- All attached visual metadata is complete and uses HTTPS.
- Every audited record has exactly one current source snapshot.
- Artist-facing records such as Confluence of Myths, Berlinale Talents, NEMO and PhotoVogue MENA Panorama remain searchable.
- Protected AOCA, AISS, Irish Workspace, NEA solicitations and UNESCO Host City records are excluded from ordinary artist search.
- Archived AFCP records do not appear.
- Search ordering continues to prioritise relevance, then verified imagery, then deadline and title.
- Existing UI code falls back safely when an external image fails.

## Remaining work

The remaining 47 active fallback records are not “unsearched.” They have documented unavailable, candidate or rights-review outcomes. The largest readiness gaps remaining across the active catalog are:

- 37 records without confirmed structured requirement rows.
- 20 records without confirmed structured eligibility rules.
- 43 records without a verified public contact email.
- 34 records with financial terms not yet fully verified.
- 81 records whose full legal or rights terms remain incomplete.

These records should remain in the enrichment and reverification workflow; one-click preparation must stay disabled where a blocking requirement, legal clause or external-form question is unresolved.
