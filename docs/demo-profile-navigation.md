# Guided demo profile navigation

The guided demo now keeps two forms of context separate:

- **Artist profile:** the polished, public-facing Creative Passport used to understand the artist's practice, selected works, professional history, and approved contact context.
- **Submission record:** the institution-facing application record used for completeness, reviewer progress, internal notes, and decision history.

Every synthetic submission artist resolves to an artist profile route. Generated profile details are explicitly synthetic demo content and do not represent real people or verified activity.

A persistent **Back to Demo** control appears only while KLEIO is in demo mode and returns the visitor to `/demo/` without changing live-account navigation.
